## Users

- **Buyers / landing visitors:** maintenance managers and plant managers at industrial companies
  (first pilot: a cookware manufacturer in Argentina). They evaluate the product at a desk or on
  a phone between shifts, and need to trust that it works on a real shop floor.
- **Supervisors (dashboard):** the maintenance lead of one plant. Reviews incidents, operators and
  the plant's technical knowledge base.
- **Operators:** shop-floor technicians. They never see the web: they report incidents by WhatsApp
  (text, voice note, photo) and get answers from the agent.

## Product Purpose

Actus captures the know-how of a plant's maintenance technicians and gives it back when it's
needed. Operators report and solve incidents through WhatsApp with an AI agent that uses the
plant's manuals and past cases; supervisors get visibility and a knowledge base that doesn't leave
when a senior technician does. Success for the landing: a plant manager books a demo.

## Brand Personality

Technological and precise. The AI is visible (motion, a living background, an agent that
answers) but the tone is industrial: concrete, serious, no hype. Confident expertise, spoken in
plain Spanish (es-AR, "usted" in marketing copy).

## Anti-references

- The current landing: rounded "friendly" type (Baloo, Nunito), gradient text, a mix of
  Aceternity / Magic UI snippets with no shared language, effects competing on every section.
- Generic AI-SaaS: purple glows, three identical feature cards, invented metrics.
- Anything playful or childish; it has to be credible in front of a plant manager.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the product working.** The WhatsApp conversation and the real dashboard are the hero
   imagery; no abstract blobs standing in for the product.
2. **Precision over decoration.** Every animation explains something (a message arriving, knowledge
   being captured). If it doesn't, it goes.
3. **Speak the plant's language.** Concrete maintenance scenarios, real claims only.
4. **One strong idea per fold.** Long scroll, deliberate pacing, short copy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback. Mobile-first performance:
visitors may be on a mid-range Android on a plant network.
